Is Xander Jane Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Vancouver, British Columbia

What we found

Designer jewellery label started in 2019 by Alex Jane, working out of downtown Vancouver. Began by contracting manufacturing out and has since brought it in house. Per a JCK interview, Jane also opened the Vancouver designer resale boutique Modaselle in 2015 — note that other sources name Natalie Ivanova as Modaselle's founder, so the two entries may need reconciling.

Where the products come from
