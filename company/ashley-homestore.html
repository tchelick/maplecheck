Is Ashley HomeStore Canadian-owned?

US-owned · Arcadia, Wisconsin (brand) — Canadian stores independently licensed

What we found

The Ashley HomeStore brand and this shared Canadian site belong to Ashley Furniture Industries, a privately held US company owned by the Wanek family, headquartered in Arcadia, Wisconsin. As with Chevron/Parkland, the brand is US but the stores are not all directly US-run: Canadian locations are operated under licence by independent regional operators, the largest being The Dufresne Group, a family-owned Winnipeg company (39 stores, BC to Ontario) — its own materials call itself '100% owned and operated by Canadians,' which is true of the licensee, not the brand. Ownership is recorded as US because that is who owns the brand and this website; the Canadian licensee relationship is noted so it isn't mistaken for full Canadian ownership.
