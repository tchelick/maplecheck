Is Kit and Ace Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Vancouver, British Columbia

What we found

Canadian-owned, though not by the family that started it. Founded in Vancouver in 2014 by Shannon and JJ Wilson of the Lululemon family, sold in 2018 to then-CEO George Tsogas, and acquired in July 2023 by Unity Brands Inc. — the Canadian group of Joe Mimran, Frank Rocchetti and David Lui, the same investors behind Mastermind Toys.
