Is The Body Shop Canadian-owned?

Not Canadian, but not US-owned either · London, United Kingdom

What we found

Resolved: after the 2024 collapse into administration, a consortium led by Aurea Group — the London growth-capital firm co-founded by Mike Jatania and Paul Raphael — completed the purchase of The Body Shop's international assets in September 2024, including the North American business. Jatania is executive chairman. British-owned; not US-owned and not Canadian.
