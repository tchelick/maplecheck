Is Blondo Canadian-owned?

US-owned · Long Island City, New York (parent)

What we found

Canadian-founded, US-owned. Blondo is a Quebec boot brand that goes back to 1910. Steven Madden, Ltd. (NASDAQ: SHOO) bought the Blondo intellectual property and related assets from Montreal's Regence Footwear in January 2015 (Steve Madden's own press release, plus trade coverage). Steve Madden's FY2025 10-K still lists Blondo among its footwear brands. Earlier signs pointing to Caleres were wrong; the owner is Steve Madden.

Canadian alternatives

  • La Canadienne (Montreal)
  • Kamik (Genfoot, Montreal)
  • Aldo (Montreal)
