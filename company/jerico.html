Is JERICO Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Greater Toronto Area, Ontario

What we found

Family-owned since 1987, started by two brothers and two sisters, making basics — t-shirts, sweatshirts, sweatpants, hoodies — entirely in Canada. It kept domestic production after import quotas were lifted in 2001, which is when most of the Canadian garment industry moved offshore; that decision is why it is one of the few remaining Canadian-made basics manufacturers.
