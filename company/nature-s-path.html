Is Nature's Path Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Richmond, British Columbia

What we found

Privately held and family-owned, founded in 1985 by Arran and Ratana Stephens and now led by the next generation. North America's largest producer of certified organic breakfast and snack foods; also owns EnviroKidz, Que Pasa, Anita's Organic Mill and Love Child Organics. Deliberately independent — the family has publicly declined acquisition offers.
