Is Roots Canadian-owned?

US-owned · Toronto, Ontario (operations) / New York, New York (Marquee Brands)

⚠ Still being verified — see what we found below.

What we found

Canadian-founded in 1973 and still run from Toronto, but now US-owned. Marquee Brands, a New York brand-management company, completed its go-private acquisition of Roots in late October 2026 at C$4.10 per share in cash, under the plan of arrangement announced 21 August 2026; Chain Store Age and FashionNetwork (28 October 2026) both reported the closing, and CEO Meghan Roach stepped down at completion. Roots is delisting from the TSX and ceasing to be a reporting issuer. Toronto-based JM&A Design & Development (Joe Mimran and Frank Rocchetti), Marquee's operating partner, runs design, manufacturing, distribution and retail, and the company says Roots continues to be operated in Canada. Before the sale, New York private equity firm Searchlight Capital had controlled Roots since 2015. Still owed: the exact closing date from Roots' own release or the TSX delisting notice, which this check could not open.
