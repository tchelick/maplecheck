What we found

Old Dutch Foods began in Minnesota, where the privately held US parent, Old Dutch Foods Inc., is based in Roseville. Its Canadian arm, Old Dutch Foods Ltd., has had its head office and a chip plant in Winnipeg since 1954 and runs several Canadian plants. The Canadian company is a separate legal entity, but no public source found says who owns it: the US parent, the same family, or someone else. No ownership flag is assigned until that is confirmed through a registry or the company's own statement.
