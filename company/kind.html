Is KIND Canadian-owned?

US-owned · McLean, Virginia (parent)

What we found

Founded by Daniel Lubetzky in New York in 2004, so never Canadian. Owned by Mars, Incorporated, the privately held US confectioner, which took a minority stake in 2017 and acquired KIND North America outright in November 2020 (Mars's announcement, plus Canadian Grocer and FoodNavigator coverage). Often shelved among Canadian products, but American.

Canadian alternatives

  • Nature's Path (Canadian, family-owned, makes granola bars)
