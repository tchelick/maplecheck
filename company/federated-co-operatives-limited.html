Is Federated Co-operatives Limited Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Saskatoon, Saskatchewan

What we found

Wholesaling and manufacturing co-operative that supplies and is owned by roughly 160 independent local retail co-operatives across Western Canada and into the Arctic — the Co-operative Retailing System (grocery stores, gas bars, hardware and agro centres, pharmacies). Formed 1944 from a merger of the Saskatchewan Co-operative Wholesale Society and Consumers' Co-operative Refineries Limited. Ownership structure and founding confirmed by Wikipedia (citing FCL's own corporate history) and the Encyclopedia of Saskatchewan, agreeing with FCL's own 'Our Story' page.
