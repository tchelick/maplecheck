Is House of Knives Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Burnaby, British Columbia

What we found

Cutlery and kitchen tool retailer established 1979. Allan Eng bought a Victoria franchise location in 1987, he and his brother Andre took the BC franchise rights in 1992, and in 2011 they bought the national rights outright from the original corporate owners — so it has been fully Canadian family-owned since then. Andre Eng is president.
