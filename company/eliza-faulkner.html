Is Eliza Faulkner Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Montreal, Quebec

What we found

Eliza Faulkner Designs Inc., the namesake label launched in 2012 by the Central Saint Martins-trained designer after working at Erdem, Roland Mouret and Zandra Rhodes in London. Designed and produced in Montreal, with deadstock fabric used where possible. Independently owned; a two-time Canadian Arts and Fashion Awards nominee.
