Is Joseph Ribkoff Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Dorval, Quebec

What we found

Founded in Montreal in 1957 and still headquartered in Quebec, where over 400 of its roughly 500 employees work, with the design studio, production and warehouse on site. Sold in 62 countries through 3,500+ retail partners. The company states the majority of its product is manufactured in Canada and Europe, so not exclusively Canadian-made.
