Is Reigning Champ Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Vancouver, BC

What we found

Vancouver athletic-wear brand that both designs and manufactures its products. Aritzia (TSX: ATZ) bought a 75% stake in June 2021 at an enterprise value of about $63 million, with the remaining management stake set to convert into Aritzia shares in instalments from 2024 to 2026 (Aritzia's own release, plus Canadian Press coverage). That makes it Canadian-owned through Aritzia. Where it manufactures today is not stated publicly, so made-in is left unset. Note that this site charged USD to Canadian visitors when checked in October 2026, and reigningchamp.ca is a parked domain listed for sale, not theirs.
