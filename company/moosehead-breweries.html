Is Moosehead Breweries Canadian-owned?

Canadian-owned · Saint John, New Brunswick

What we found

Privately held by the Oland family since 1867, now in its sixth generation — widely described as the last major Canadian-owned brewery, after Molson, Labatt and Sleeman all passed into foreign ownership. Independent of any multinational parent. Confirmed via the company's own materials plus independent brewing-industry profiles.
