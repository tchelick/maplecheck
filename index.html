Why ownership, not branding

A "Truly Canadian" storefront can be owned by a US private equity firm. An "iconic Canadian brand" can have been owned by an American company for over a decade. Names, flags, and marketing copy tell you what a company wants you to believe. Corporate filings and acquisition records tell you who actually gets the money. We research the second kind of fact — and we say plainly when we're not sure yet.
